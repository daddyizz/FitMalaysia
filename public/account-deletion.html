Delete your FitMalaysia account and data

This page applies to FitMalaysia, published by daddyizz.

Delete from the app

  1. Open FitMalaysia and sign in with the Google account used for backup.
  2. Open You > Settings.
  3. Under Your Data, choose Delete Everything and confirm.

This deletes your FitMalaysia Firebase account, private cloud backup, profile, goals, workout history, weight and BMI records, water logs, favourites, achievements and local app settings. Revoking Health Connect permissions or deleting records already written to Health Connect must be done in Android Health Connect settings.

Request deletion without the app

Email dady.izz85@gmail.com from the Google email used in FitMalaysia. Use the subject “FitMalaysia account deletion”. We may ask you to confirm account ownership before deletion.

Deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days. Limited security or legal records may be retained only where required by law or to prevent abuse.
