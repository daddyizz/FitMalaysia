FitMalaysia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

FitMalaysia is a fitness companion published by daddyizz. It works in guest mode without an account and offers optional Google sign-in for cloud backup.

Fitness information on your device

FitMalaysia stores profile details, goals, weight and BMI entries, water logs, workout history, favourites, achievements and app preferences locally. In guest mode this information remains only in local app storage.

Optional Google account and cloud backup

If you choose Continue with Google, Firebase Authentication processes your Google account identifier, email address, display name and profile image. FitMalaysia stores a private backup of your FitMalaysia profile, goals, fitness logs, favourites, achievements and settings in Google Cloud Firestore so it can be restored after reinstalling or changing phones. User-specific Firestore rules restrict access to the signed-in user.

Cloud backup is optional. FitMalaysia does not request access to your Google Drive, contacts, email content or password. Firebase may process device, app, authentication, security and diagnostic information to provide these services.

Health Connect

With your permission, FitMalaysia reads daily step totals and weight and writes workouts completed in FitMalaysia. This information is used only for fitness features, not advertising, marketing or sale. Access can be revoked in Android settings.

Advertising and consent

FitMalaysia shows a limited Google Mobile Ads banner. Google may process IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, fraud prevention and analytics. Fitness and Health Connect information is not shared for personalised advertising. Where required, Google's consent flow is shown and eligible choices can be revisited in Settings > Privacy options.

Retention and deletion

Guest data remains until you use Settings > Delete everything, clear app storage or uninstall. For signed-in users, Settings > Delete everything removes local data, the cloud backup and the FitMalaysia account. Signing out alone does not delete the backup.

See the account deletion page for detailed steps.

Health information and children

FitMalaysia provides general fitness and nutrition education only. It is not medical advice or a medical device. FitMalaysia is not designed for children under 13.
