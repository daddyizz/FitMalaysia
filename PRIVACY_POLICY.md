# FitMalaysia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

FitMalaysia is a fitness companion published by daddyizz. It works in guest mode without an account and also offers optional Google sign-in for cloud backup. This policy explains how information is handled when using the Android app.

## Fitness information stored on your device

FitMalaysia stores profile details, goals, weight and BMI entries, water logs, workout history, favourites, achievements and app preferences locally on the device. In guest mode this fitness information remains only in the app's local storage.

## Optional Google account and cloud backup

If you choose **Continue with Google**, Firebase Authentication processes your Google account identifier, email address, display name and profile image to sign you in. FitMalaysia then stores a private backup of your FitMalaysia profile, goals, fitness logs, favourites, achievements and settings in Google Cloud Firestore. The backup is used to restore your data after reinstalling the app or moving to another phone. It is linked to your Firebase user ID and Firestore security rules restrict access to that signed-in user.

Cloud backup is optional. You may sign out and continue using the local data on your device. FitMalaysia does not request access to your Google Drive, contacts, email or password.

Firebase may process device, app, authentication, security and diagnostic information to provide authentication, cloud storage, fraud prevention and service reliability. Firebase data may be processed on Google infrastructure outside your country subject to Google's safeguards.

## Health Connect

Health Connect is optional and requires your permission. If connected, FitMalaysia reads daily step totals and weight entries, and writes workouts completed in FitMalaysia. This information is used only to show your progress, update your weight record and save completed workouts in Health Connect. FitMalaysia does not use Health Connect data for advertising, marketing or sale. You can revoke Health Connect permissions in Android settings at any time.

## Advertising

FitMalaysia shows a limited banner advertisement on the Home screen using Google Mobile Ads. Google Mobile Ads may collect and process technical information, such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information, for advertising, fraud prevention and analytics. Google's policies and controls apply to this processing. Fitness and Health Connect information is not shared with Google Mobile Ads for personalised advertising.

## Consent and advertising choices

Where required, including in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, FitMalaysia presents Google's consent flow before requesting ads. Where available, you can revisit your advertising choices through **Settings > Privacy options**. You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID through Android system settings.

## Data retention and deletion

Guest data remains on your device until you use **Settings > Delete everything**, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Signed-in cloud backups remain until you choose **Settings > Delete everything**, which deletes the cloud backup, FitMalaysia account and local data. Signing out alone does not delete either local data or the backup.

You may also request account deletion through the repository issue tracker linked below. We may retain limited records where required for security, legal compliance or resolving abuse, but FitMalaysia does not intentionally keep a separate copy of deleted fitness backup data.

## Health information

FitMalaysia provides general fitness and nutrition education only. It is not medical advice and is not a medical device. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional for personal health guidance.

## Children

FitMalaysia is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version is published in the [FitMalaysia repository](https://github.com/daddyizz/FitMalaysia). For privacy questions, access requests or deletion support, open an issue through the repository's [issue tracker](https://github.com/daddyizz/FitMalaysia/issues).
