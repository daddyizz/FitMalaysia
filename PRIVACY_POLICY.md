# FitMalaysia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

FitMalaysia is an offline fitness companion published by daddyizz. This policy explains how information is handled when using the Android app.

## Fitness information stored on your device

FitMalaysia stores profile details, weight and BMI entries, water logs, workout history, favourites, achievements and app preferences locally on the device. The app does not require an account and does not send this fitness information to a FitMalaysia server.

You can remove this information in the app through **Settings > Delete everything**. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app storage.

## Advertising

FitMalaysia shows a limited banner advertisement on the Home screen using Google Mobile Ads. Google Mobile Ads may collect and process technical information, such as IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information, for advertising, fraud prevention and analytics. Google’s policies and controls apply to this processing.

## Consent and advertising choices

Where required, including in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, FitMalaysia presents Google’s consent flow before requesting ads. Where available, you can revisit your advertising choices through **Settings > Privacy options**. You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID through Android system settings.

## Health information

FitMalaysia provides general fitness and nutrition education only. It is not medical advice and is not a medical device. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional for personal health guidance.

## Children

FitMalaysia is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version is published in the [FitMalaysia repository](https://github.com/daddyizz/FitMalaysia). For privacy questions or requests, open an issue through the repository’s [issue tracker](https://github.com/daddyizz/FitMalaysia/issues).
