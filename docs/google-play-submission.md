### Full description

FitMalaysia helps you build a fitness routine that fits your day.

Choose from 96 guided workouts for different levels, goals and equipment. Each session includes a focused timer, movement guidance and a realistic visual to help you stay on track from warm-up to finish.

MAKE PROGRESS YOUR WAY

• Follow workouts for beginner, intermediate and advanced levels
• Use the timer, pause when you need to and keep moving at your own pace
• Track workout history, streaks, achievements, weight and BMI
• Set water targets and log your daily progress
• Explore practical nutrition guides for protein, carbohydrates, fruits and more
• Save favourite workouts and choose the next session when you are ready

OPTIONAL, PRIVATE CONNECTED FEATURES

FitMalaysia works in guest mode without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, you can back up and restore your own FitMalaysia progress across devices. You can also choose to connect Health Connect to read your steps and weight, and to write completed FitMalaysia workouts. These features are optional and can be disconnected at any time.

FitMalaysia is built for consistent, sustainable movement. Start where you are, move at your pace and celebrate every step forward.

FitMalaysia provides general fitness and nutrition education only. It is not medical advice or a medical device.

## App content answers

### App access

* **All or some functionality is restricted:** No.
* **Reviewer instructions:** FitMalaysia is fully usable in guest mode. Google sign-in, cloud backup and Health Connect are optional; no test account or special credentials are required.

### Ads

* **Does the app contain ads?** Yes.
* The app contains limited banner ads from Google Mobile Ads at natural breaks in the experience. EEA/UK consent options are available from Settings when required.

### Target audience and content

* The app is not designed for children under 13.
* Select the audience bands that match the intended fitness audience. Do not select child-directed audiences.
* Complete the content rating questionnaire truthfully as a fitness and nutrition education app. It does not contain violence, gambling, sexual content, user-to-user messaging or user-generated content.

### Health apps declaration

* **Health feature:** Health and fitness.
* **Health Connect data accessed:** steps (read), weight (read), workouts/exercise sessions (write).
* **Suggested explanation:** “FitMalaysia lets a user optionally connect Health Connect. With explicit user permission, it reads the user’s daily step total and latest weight to show personal fitness progress in the app. When the user completes a FitMalaysia workout, the app can write that completed workout session to Health Connect so the user’s health record stays up to date. This data is used only for the user-facing fitness features, never for advertising or sale.”

## Data Safety — recommended answers

Complete this only for the behaviour in the release bundle. The app contains Google Mobile Ads, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Sign-In and optional Health Connect.

### First questions

* **Does the app collect or share any required user data types?** Yes.
* **Is data encrypted in transit?** Yes, for the network services used by the app.
* **Can users request deletion?** Yes. In-app: **You → Settings → Delete everything**. Web: https://fitmalaysia-134fe.web.app/account-deletion.html

### Data types to declare

| Play data type | Collected / shared | Purpose | Notes for the form |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Collected, optional; not used for advertising | Account management, app functionality | Google display name is used only when the user chooses Google sign-in/cloud backup. |
| Email address | Collected, optional; not used for advertising | Account management | Used for optional Google sign-in and support/deletion verification. |
| User IDs | Collected, optional; not used for advertising | Account management, app functionality | Firebase user ID is used to secure the user’s own cloud backup. |
| Health and fitness | Collected, optional; not used for advertising | App functionality | Optional cloud backup can contain fitness logs; optional Health Connect reads steps/weight and writes completed workouts. Also complete the separate Health apps declaration. |
| Approximate location | Collected and shared by the ads SDK | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention | Google Mobile Ads may derive approximate location from IP address. |
| App interactions | Collected and shared by the ads SDK | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention | Includes ad/app interaction signals handled by Google Mobile Ads. |
| App info and performance — diagnostics | Collected and shared by the ads SDK | Analytics, fraud prevention | Google Mobile Ads SDK diagnostics. |
| Device or other IDs | Collected and shared by the ads SDK | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention | Includes Android advertising ID and app-set ID where applicable. |

For the four advertising rows, select the form’s “shared” choices as required by the current Google Mobile Ads SDK disclosure. For account and fitness data, select “optional” where the form asks whether collection is required; guest mode does not require an account or Health Connect.

## Release track recommendation

1. Create the app in Play Console using package `com.daddyizz.fitmalaysia`.
2. Upload `build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab` to **Internal testing** first.
3. Complete Store listing, App content, Data Safety and the Health apps declaration using this pack.
4. Install the internal-test build from Google Play on the physical phone and test Google sign-in, Health Connect, consent choices, ads and account deletion.
5. Move to **Closed testing** only after the internal build is clean; then create the Production release.
